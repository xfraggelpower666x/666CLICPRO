## Why relevant
666STREAM already has strong project-scoped repository continuity, chat-local foreground, project maps and temporal LiveCircle semantics. A development-return notice to CLIC would let CLIC keep its 666STREAM system card current and propagate generalized learning without re-auditing the whole STREAM repo every time.
