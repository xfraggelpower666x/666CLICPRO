## Core design conclusion

Track Design must not merely expose capabilities. Before a track build, the current native Track Design must be understood as a current capability-and-relation landscape and must resolve which capabilities are relevant for the specific track.

→ CURRENT_RELATION_MAP

## Adaptive template contract

Static revision-bound templates are forbidden as the long-term design target.

CURRENT_TRACK_DESIGN_ADAPTIVE_TEMPLATE_CONTRACT must:
- derive from current native Track Design, not a frozen revision snapshot;
- revalidate on valid native Track Design evolution;
- preserve newer valid evolution and supersession;
- invalidate stale derived views;
- separate stable visual grammar from dynamic current semantic content;
- never become musical or native LYVRA decision authority.

Currentness binding should consider:
- current logical Track Design revision;
- current state/provider carriers;
- current guard carriers;
- current continuity/handoff carriers;
- current Whole↔Track relations;
- current renderer context;
- current output contract;
- current source evidence.

STALE_TEMPLATE =
CURRENT_NATIVE_EVOLUTION > TEMPLATE_DERIVATION

STALE_TEMPLATE → TRACK_READY=false → rehydrate current → resolve supersession → re-derive → recheck.

## Loudness and mastering

INTENSITY_GROWTH != CONTINUOUS_LOUDNESS_GROWTH

Required conceptual bindings:
MAX_USABLE_LOUDNESS_RELATION
MASTERING_POWER_RELATION
LONG_TERM_LOUDNESS_RAMP_GUARD

Energy may rise through:
density
spectral motion
transient force
psychoacoustic pressure
arrangement contrast
vocal development
hook/mantra development
controlled chaos

Average master level must not become a continuous beginning-to-end gain ramp.

LOUDNESS_OVERSTEER:
late average level is materially higher,
rise persists across the track,
and the rise is not structurally necessary.

Local heal:
- preserve musical energy curve;
- reduce long-term loudness slope;
- restore transient contrast;
- preserve headroom;
- preserve low-end definition;
- preserve final impact;
- do not flatten dynamics.

Creator-provided rendered audio may be behavioral evidence for loudness slope, motor continuity, dynamic contrast and ending behavior, but is not a universal renderer guarantee.
