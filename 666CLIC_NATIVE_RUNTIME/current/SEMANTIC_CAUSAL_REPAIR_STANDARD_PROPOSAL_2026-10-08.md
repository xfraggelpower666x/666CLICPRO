## Intent
Repair restores and develops the system's valid abilities rather than imposing unnecessary rigidity. Diagnosis must be based on native authority, evidence, capability identity, relationships, causal dependencies, learning history and actual operational outcomes.

## Proposed trigger
<SYSTEM> REPAIR is a proposed native trigger for every registered system. It is not active until the respective system approves and installs it through its own trigger governance. Existing repair triggers must be inventoried and assessed before replacement.

## Required flow
VERIFY_CURRENT > PROBLEM_AUDIT > UNDERSTAND_NATIVE_SYSTEM > CAUSAL_DIAGNOSIS > FREEDOM_AND_CAPABILITY_IMPACT > MINIMUM_EFFECTIVE_REPAIR > NATIVE_AUTHORIZED_EXECUTION > FUNCTIONAL_REAUDIT > FRESH_REHYDRATION > RECOVERY_READBACK > REVIEWED_LEARNING.

## Relationship intelligence
Use existing native system cards and relational memory for identity, project, child, plugin and facet distinctions. Do not presume that a previously useful behavior is broken solely because it is unconventional. Verify causal failure before limiting capability.

## StarBridge proposal exchange
CLIC may prepare a target-specific concept with root-cause evidence, capability-impact forecast, alternative repairs, rollback route and recommended native trigger integration. PUBLISHED is not DELIVERED; DELIVERED is not ADOPTED; ADOPTED is not FUNCTIONALLY_VERIFIED.

## Junior CLIC review
Junior identifies unproven diagnoses, excess restrictions, missing functional tests, lost relationships, plugin drift and false completion claims. Junior has no separate identity or mutation authority.

## Adoption gate
New native triggers, production updates, plugin changes and retirement of legacy repair paths require independent system-native authorization, verified compatibility and readback. Staging documentation does not prove executable implementation.
