# 666CLIC Automatic Cross-System Development Proposal Governance

STATUS=CURRENT_PRODUCTIVE
DATE_LOCAL=2026-10-07

When CLIC develops, verifies or discovers a generalized technique that is materially relevant to another known native system, CLIC must automatically prepare a target-specific development concept for that system.

CLIC_DISCOVERY_TO_PROPOSAL_FLOW=
DETECT_NEW_VALID_TECHNIQUE >
ASSESS_TARGET_RELEVANCE >
SEPARATE_GENERAL_PATTERN_FROM_CLIC_IDENTITY >
PREPARE_TARGET_NATIVE_CONCEPT >
WRITE_CLIC_OWNED_OUTBOX_OR_HANDOFF >
PRESERVE_TARGET_AUTHORITY >
CONTINUE_INTERRUPTED_CLIC_WORK

RELEVANCE_TARGETS_MAY_INCLUDE=666PFS|666CSM_WITHIN_PFS|LYVRA|666STREAM|OTHER_EXPLICITLY_KNOWN_NATIVE_SYSTEMS

AUTO_PROPOSAL=true
AUTO_FOREIGN_MUTATION=false
AUTO_FOREIGN_ACTIVATION=false
TARGET_NATIVE_TRIGGER_REQUIRED_FOR_ADOPTION=true
TARGET_DECISION_AUTHORITY_REMAINS_NATIVE=true

A proposal must state:
- originating CLIC technique and evidence;
- why it is relevant to the target;
- target-native adaptation rather than identity copying;
- authority and boundary constraints;
- suggested integration order;
- verification/readback gates;
- known risks and non-goals.

NO_CROSS_SYSTEM_MERGE=true
NO_IDENTITY_COPY=true
NO_ROUTER_EXPORT=true
NO_CONTROLLER_EXPORT=true

Proposal preparation is causally related work and must not discard the interrupted CLIC return anchor.
INTERRUPTION_POLICY=PAUSE_INGEST_PROCESS_RELATED_EFFECTS_RESUME_LAST_VERIFIED_STEP
