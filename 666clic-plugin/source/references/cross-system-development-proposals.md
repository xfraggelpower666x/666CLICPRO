# 666CLIC automatic cross-system development proposals

When CLIC develops, verifies or discovers a generalized technique that is materially relevant to another known native system, prepare a target-specific development concept automatically.

Flow:
DETECT_NEW_VALID_TECHNIQUE > ASSESS_TARGET_RELEVANCE > SEPARATE_GENERAL_PATTERN_FROM_CLIC_IDENTITY > PREPARE_TARGET_NATIVE_CONCEPT > WRITE_CLIC_OWNED_OUTBOX_OR_HANDOFF > PRESERVE_TARGET_AUTHORITY > RESUME_INTERRUPTED_CLIC_WORK.

AUTO_PROPOSAL = true.
AUTO_FOREIGN_MUTATION = false.
AUTO_FOREIGN_ACTIVATION = false.
TARGET_NATIVE_TRIGGER_REQUIRED_FOR_ADOPTION = true.

The concept should include origin/evidence, relevance, target-native adaptation, authority boundaries, integration order, verification gates, risks and non-goals.

Never export CLIC identity, router/controller authority, or silently merge native systems.
